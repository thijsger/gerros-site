Release notes, straight from the store.

531 versions shipped across 51 apps. The latest change per app, newest first.

28 Oct 2026

RallyPoint 2.7.1

Undo now fully restores deuce and match stats, tiebreaks show side changes, switching sport mid-match asks for confirmation, match duration stops at match point, and the per-set page shows all five sets.

28 Oct 2026

ShiftPay 1.6.0

Overtime can now count per week (for example after 40 h), the threshold goes up to 168 h, and typing 50 as the overtime rate now means time and a half (150%).

28 Oct 2026

Reminders+ 1.5.1

BreathGym 1.5.0

Speedometer Pro 1.5.3

MorseTap 1.2.4
