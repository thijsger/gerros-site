Release notes, straight from the store.

Convertr 2.0.2

Cleaner layout on Instinct watches: the unit is fully visible and the keypad shows its DEL and OK keys. Exact absolute zero in temperature conversion, more precise number entry, clearer currency status and sturdier storage handling.

Triptych 1.4.2

Sub-dial values and labels now stay inside their rings on smaller screens. Temperature and distance follow your watch units, and always-on mode is gentler on MIP screens.

Strike 1.0.5
