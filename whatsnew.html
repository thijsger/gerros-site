What's new

Release notes, straight from the store.

198 versions shipped across 27 apps. The latest change per app, newest first.

6 Sep 2026

Sobr 2.1

  • A drink logged on an earlier day, or removed from one, now updates that day in the 30-day and monthly charts instead of today
6 Sep 2026

Caffi 2.1

  • With a long half-life set (10 to 12 h) doses no longer vanish from the curve after 36 hours; the window now scales with your half-life
  • The "you can sleep" notification follows your bedtime setting instead of a fixed 18:00 to 06:00 window
  • Changing half-life, threshold or bedtime from the phone re-plans the notification immediately
  • A dose logged on an earlier day, or removed from one, updates that day in the charts instead of today
6 Sep 2026

Smokeless 1.6

  • The quit moment is only set when you open the app, never by the glance or a background check
  • Moving the quit moment later now banks your streak and counts as a new attempt, like "quit again now" does
  • The daily reminder keeps its hour across the daylight-saving change
6 Sep 2026

BreathGym 1.4

  • Holds are now timed on the wall clock: a notification or wrist gesture during a hold no longer costs you seconds, so a record is a record
  • Ending a hold early rounds the time to the nearest second instead of down
6 Sep 2026

Reminders+ 1.3

  • A scheduled reminder ticked off up to six hours early now counts for that occurrence instead of turning red at the set time
  • Changing the interval of a reminder keeps "last done"; it no longer starts over from zero
  • Overdue reminders now remind you again every hour until you tick them off
6 Sep 2026

RallyPoint 2.6

  • Undo now also restores who serves and the chosen first servers
  • Undo works on small-screen watches too (Forerunner 55 and similar), with a shorter history
  • New in the match menu: "Touchscreen" on/off and "Buttons", which flips UP/DOWN scoring for those who see "You" at the bottom
6 Sep 2026

Convertr 2.0

  • All conversions now calculate in double precision: terabytes, large currency amounts and data rates keep every digit
  • Data units renamed to KiB, MiB, GiB and TiB to make clear they are binary; data rates stay decimal
  • When rates cannot be refreshed you now see "no connection, tap to retry"
6 Sep 2026

Fasted 1.9

  • Sun mode: a discarded fast no longer restarts itself the same day
  • Days are read from the calendar, so fasts ending around midnight stay on the right day across daylight-saving changes and travel
  • An end time before the start time is refused instead of logging a zero-length fast
6 Sep 2026

Speedometer Pro 1.4

  • The odometer no longer creeps up while standing still: distance only counts above the idle threshold and with a usable GPS fix
  • Settings changed in the Connect IQ phone app now apply again, even after a setting was changed on the watch
  • New option "Trip on open": continue the previous trip or start fresh every time
  • Less battery at rest: the needle animation slows down while nothing moves
  • Tip for Forerunner 970 and other newer watches: the app lives in the activity list, next to your sports
6 Sep 2026

SpotSave 1.8

  • The watch's last known position is used as a fallback right away, so a quick save no longer fails while GPS is still searching
  • A weak GPS fix now saves with a warning instead of silently, and the status shows "GPS weak"
  • "Ago" shows days after two days instead of "49h"
6 Sep 2026

Podium 1.0.3

  • "Usually +1:20" now follows the talk itself, so renaming a talk keeps its history
  • Chapters you did not tap through show as unknown in the summary instead of "on time"
  • Glance chapter count matches the app
6 Sep 2026

Listo 2.0.3

  • Unticking a product now undoes the tick: it no longer counts as bought or as frequently bought
  • Fewer storage writes at start-up
6 Sep 2026

Spent 1.0.3

  • Switching between monthly and weekly budget no longer writes a bogus history entry
  • Periods you missed while the app was closed are each recorded separately
  • Glance now works out today's allowance itself on later days
  • Editing or deleting an expense from a closed period updates that period's history
6 Sep 2026

ShiftPay 1.4

  • A night shift is now logged on the day it started, not the morning it ended
  • Day totals and 7-day sums stay correct across the daylight-saving change
