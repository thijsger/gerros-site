How do I install a Connect IQ app?

Open its Connect IQ store page, buy the app and choose Install. Garmin Connect syncs it to a compatible watch.

Why does an app ask for a permission?

A permission unlocks a watch feature the app needs, such as GPS, heart rate or storage. GerrOS apps do not send that data to us.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by the store. Contact Garmin for Connect IQ purchases or Google for Google Play purchases.

Which watches are supported?

Compatibility differs by app. Open an app page here for its device count, then use the store page to check your exact model.

Which languages are included?

Every app includes English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish. WordClock supports ten languages.
