A one-person studio in the Netherlands.

GerrOS is run by Thijs Gerritsen from Den Dolder. Every app starts as something he wanted on his own watch, gets built end to end, and is then kept up: reviews are answered, bugs are fixed and features arrive in updates.

It began with a score tracker for padel. The eight apps on this site are the ones rebuilt in the current design: one typeface, large clear numbers, a ring round the edge and every menu as a page with big buttons.

How the apps are made

Built for a glance

A watch is read in half a second. Each app leads with the one number that matters and keeps the rest a swipe away.

Real arithmetic inside

A caffeine curve, a blood-alcohol estimate that knows when you are sober, a fasting clock that knows your window, a cycle forecast. The apps plan and predict; they do not only count.

Yours stays on the watch

No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your data lives on the device, and every app works without a phone nearby.

Buttons and touch

Every app works on a touch screen and with the buttons alone, and every menu is a page with large buttons.

Kept alive

Reviews get a reply and bugs get fixed. The apps on this site have had 131 versions between them so far.
