Cue

Snooker and pool scoring on your watch.

★ 5.0 · 1 review · version 1.0.0

Open in the Connect IQ Store

Cue keeps the score of your snooker or pool match on your wrist, so the table stays clear and nobody argues about the count. Tap the ball you potted, tap a foul, and Cue handles the rest: whose turn it is, which ball is on, the running break, and how many points are still on the table.

The numbers that matter in a tight frame are always on screen. Points remaining, whether you are on a frame ball, and exactly how many snookers you need. When the reds are gone the colours come off in order automatically, and a tie on the black re-spots it. Pool players get a clean race counter for 8-ball and 9-ball: one tap per rack, progress bars towards the race.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Snooker scoring: tap the ball, Cue knows whether it is legal and what it is worth
  • Automatic ball-on: reds and colours alternate, then yellow to black in sequence
  • Points remaining, frame ball, snookers needed and difference at a glance
  • Fouls from 4 to 7 with the correct award, free ball and re-spot on a tie
  • Undo any pot or foul, end a break with a swipe
  • Best of 3, 5, 7, 9 and more, frame dots under each player
  • 15, 10 or 6 reds for short frames
  • 8-ball and 9-ball race counters: tap a side for the rack
  • Stats: frames played, wins, highest break, pots and fouls per player
  • History of your last frames with score and break
  • Glance shows the live score, five languages, buttons or touch
