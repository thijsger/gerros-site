What it does

Reminders+ — everything that repeats, right on your wrist.

Some things come back. Medication every eight hours. The bins on Tuesday. Plants on Sunday. Contact lenses every month. Chain oil every two weeks. The car in for service every six months. The smoke alarm once a year.

Your phone can technically do all of that, but setting up a recurring reminder there is a chore, and half of them end up buried in a list you stop opening. Reminders+ does one thing instead: it keeps track of everything that repeats, and tells you the moment something is due, on the watch you are already wearing.

Set It Up However It Actually Repeats

  • Every interval from 15 minutes to a full year: minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years
  • Or type your own: any number of minutes, hours or days
  • Or fixed days: pick the weekdays it should land on, and the time of day
  • Or a day of the month: the 1st, the 15th, the 28th, at the hour you choose. Short months are handled for you, so the 31st becomes the last day.

See Where You Stand In One Glance

  • A status ring around the main screen, one segment per reminder: green when there is time, amber when it is coming up, red when it is due
  • The big number in the middle: how many reminders need you right now
  • The glance shows what is due without even opening the app
  • The list is sorted by urgency, so the thing that matters is always on top

It Tells You When It Is Time

  • Your watch alerts you when a reminder comes due
  • One alert per due moment, so it will not nag you in a loop
  • Switch alerts off from the bottom of the list whenever you want

Start In Seconds

Templates for the things people track most, in three groups. Vehicle: chain oil, tyre pressure, oil change, inspection, brakes, battery, wash. Home: plants, bins, bed sheets, vacuuming, water filter, smoke alarm. Health: drink water, stretch, meds, vitamins, brush head, contact lenses. Pick one and adjust the interval, or type your own name on the watch keyboard. One tap on Mark done and the countdown starts over from that moment.

Good To Know

  • Everything stays on the watch. No account, no phone connection, no sync, no data leaving your wrist.
  • Up to 25 reminders.
  • Alerts are checked in the background at the interval Connect IQ allows, so for very short intervals an alert can arrive a few minutes late.
  • Available in English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish.
