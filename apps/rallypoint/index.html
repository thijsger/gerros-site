What it does

RallyPoint — Score Tracker & Live Scoreboard for Padel, Tennis, Pickleball, Squash, Badminton & Table Tennis

Stop arguing about the score. One swipe scores the point on your Garmin watch, the live web scoreboard updates instantly on any phone, tablet or TV, and the match saves to Garmin Connect when you're done.

Live scoreboard — rallypoint.pro

  • Open rallypoint.pro on any phone, tablet, laptop or smart TV
  • Enter your unique 4-digit PIN and go live in seconds
  • Instant score updates with no refresh or delay
  • Sport-specific court rendering, serve indicator, set history and match progress
  • Ultra-realistic voice score readout in English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Español and Français
  • iPhone fullscreen mode for a clean courtside scoreboard
  • No app or account needed for spectators

Sports

  • Padel — WPT and Premier Padel rules, 4-server rotation in doubles
  • Tennis — singles and doubles, deuce, advantage and tiebreaks
  • Pickleball — rally scoring or classic side-out scoring
  • Squash — best of 5 to 11
  • Badminton — rally scoring to 21
  • Table tennis / Ping pong — best of 5 to 11

Match formats

  • 1 set, best of 3, best of 5, pro set or unlimited
  • Super-tiebreak as a deciding set
  • Golden point or full advantage
  • Tiebreaks, server rotation and side switches handled automatically

Garmin Connect and match stats

Every match saves as a Garmin activity with sport type, heart rate, calories and duration. Garmin Connect stores your workout data, while RallyPoint.pro shows the full match data: set-by-set scoreline, total points, points won, longest streak, points per minute, games, sets, tiebreaks and full set history.

Controls

  • Touch watches — swipe up for your point, swipe down for theirs, swipe left to undo
  • Button watches — UP/DOWN to score, BACK to undo, START for menu
  • Long-press to start a new match
  • Vibrates at game point, set point and match point

Languages

English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Español and Français. Change language on the watch mid-match — the live scoreboard and voice readout follow automatically.
