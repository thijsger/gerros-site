Caffi — Know your caffeine, protect your sleep.

Most caffeine apps just count down from your last cup. Caffi models the caffeine actually in your body with a real pharmacokinetic curve — both the absorption (the rise after you drink) and the elimination (the slow decay) — so the peak and the timing are realistic, not guessed.

At a glance you see how much caffeine is in your body right now, when you can sleep again, until what time you can still have a coffee, and whether you are under your daily limit.

FEATURES

  • Caffeine in your body (mg) on a ring with green, orange and red zones
  • Sleep time: the moment your level drops below your sleep threshold
  • "Coffee by": the latest time for another cup that still lets you sleep, also shown per drink
  • Curve with sleep and stimulation thresholds, bedtime line and your planned drinks
  • Timing: when and how much to take for a peak at a chosen time
  • Day planner: set your focus moments and get a drink schedule, with optional reminders
  • Taper plan: cut down step by step with a weekly budget
  • Daily limit with alerts at 80 % and 100 %
  • Trends: 14 days, 90 days, by hour of day and per month
  • Half-life test: a few questions suggest your personal half-life
  • More than 130 drinks, including coffee chains and energy drinks, plus your own shortcuts
  • Edit or delete logged drinks
  • Glance and watch face complications
  • Works on touch and button watches, in 16 languages

On older watches with little memory (fenix 5 and 6, Forerunner 245, 645 and 935, vívoactive 3, Venu Sq, Enduro) the planner, taper plan, half-life test, drinks database and the new look are not available.

MAKE IT YOURS

Set your body weight, half-life, sleep and stimulation thresholds, daily limit and bedtime. Caffi recalculates your curve right away.

Caffi suggests timings based on a general model. It is not medical advice.
