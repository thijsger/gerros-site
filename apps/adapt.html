Adapt

Calorie target that learns from your scale.

In review

Open in the Connect IQ Store

Submitted to Garmin and awaiting review. The store page opens once it is approved.

Adapt is a calorie target that learns from your scale. Log what you eat and weigh yourself when you can, and Adapt works out what your body actually burns: not a formula, but the number that explains your own weight trend against your own intake. From that it gives you one number that matters today: how much you can still eat.

Most calorie targets are a guess from a formula, and a wrong guess quietly costs you months. Adapt starts with the formula, then replaces it with your measured burn as the log grows. Your weigh-ins are smoothed into a trend line, so water swings and a big dinner do not move the target. Set a goal weight and a rate, and Adapt shows the date you get there, whether you are on track, and how much to eat to stay there.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Today page: calories left, eaten of target, a ring that fills as you eat
  • Measured burn (TDEE) from your intake and your weight trend, blended with the formula until there is enough data
  • Confidence bar shows how much of the estimate is measured
  • Weight trend: smoothed line through your weigh-ins, weekly change, 30-day chart
  • Goal: target weight and rate per week, days to go, projected date, on-track or behind
  • Daily target follows your goal and never drops below a safe floor
  • Quick keypad for meals, today's total and weight; kilograms or pounds
  • Activity level and profile from Garmin for the formula prior
  • Glance with calories left today and the fill bar
  • Five languages, works with buttons or touch
