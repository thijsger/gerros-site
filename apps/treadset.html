Treadset

Treadmill runs at belt speed.

In reviewat Garmin
1.0.0version, 25 Nov 2026
106Garmin watch models

Submitted to Garmin and waiting for review. The store link appears here once it is approved.

Treadset measures your treadmill run from the belt, not from your wrist. Set the speed the treadmill runs at with two buttons and distance, pace and splits follow from it to the metre, so a tempo run at 14.9 km/h finally shows 14.9 km/h instead of whatever the arm swing guessed.

After a minute at two speeds Treadset knows your own cadence curve. Changed the belt and forgot the watch? It notices the new cadence, suggests the speed ("12.0?") and one press rewrites the distance from that moment. Is the treadmill itself off? Time a tape mark on the belt and every metre is corrected.

What it does

Speed and incline with UP/DOWN or swipes, START switches between them. Free run, intervals by distance or time, progression runs and goal pace (pick a race distance and a time, Treadset works out the belt speed). Steps change on their own with a vibration and "Set the belt to 15.0 km/h". Splits per km or mile, laps per km or per step. Outdoor pace for the same effort on a flat road (no air resistance on the belt: the 1% rule of Jones and Doust, incline cost from Minetti). Calories from the ACSM running equation. Undo a mistaken press, keep running after an accidental stop. Week ring with a weekly distance goal, last 7 and 30 days, 8-week trend, splits of the last run, full history to edit, delete or add runs. km or miles, 16 languages. Glance with this week's distance. Saved as a treadmill run with belt distance, belt speed, incline and outdoor equivalent in Garmin Connect.

What it needs

A watch with buttons or a touchscreen; heart rate and cadence come from the watch when available. Treadset cannot run Garmin Coach workouts. Garmin Connect also keeps its own wrist-based distance next to the Treadset fields.
