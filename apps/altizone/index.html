AltiZone

Barometric altimeter with colour zones and alarms

Get it on Connect IQ ↗$1.99 / €2.49one-time purchase

What it does

Note: AltiZone opens from your watch's activity list (with your sports), not the app list — start it like you'd start a run or a hike.

Your altitude as one big number, surrounded by a color-coded zone arc. One glance tells you how high you are.

Features

  • Large altitude display with colored zone arc
  • 4 fully customizable zones (boundaries + colors)
  • Vertical speed: green when climbing, red when descending
  • Swipe left/right: show vertical speed as the big number
  • Speed units: m/s, m/min, km/h (or ft/s, ft/min, mph)
  • 3 vibration alerts: altitude, direction (up/down/both), pulses
  • Total ascent, max altitude and recording time on screen

Calibration

  • Type the exact altitude on an on-watch keypad
  • Set to zero
  • Auto: follow the watch's own barometer

Recording

  • Saves a full activity with altitude profile and GPS track
  • 13 activity types: hiking, mountaineering, climbing, skiing, snowboarding, trail running, mountain biking and more

Settings

  • Everything adjustable on the watch and in Garmin Connect
  • Meters or feet

Note: this app uses the barometric sensor and is intended for outdoor and mountain sports. It is not designed for skydiving, BASE jumping, paragliding or other extreme flight sports and must not be used as a safety device.
