TRIPTYCH - HUD FACE

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A triptych is a work of art in three panels. This face gives you exactly that: three clean dials below a big, crisp time display - on a pure black background that looks stunning on AMOLED and saves battery.

THREE DIALS. YOUR CHOICE.

Pick what each dial shows, straight from the Garmin Connect app on your phone:

  • Smart (automatic): Body Battery in the morning and evening when recovery matters, steps during the day
  • Heart rate
  • Steps
  • Body Battery
  • Battery: shown in DAYS remaining, not a meaningless percentage
  • Calories
  • Distance
  • Stress

DESIGNED FOR EVERY GLANCE

  • Large digital time with date and current temperature
  • A live second marker that runs along the minute track
  • No clutter, no gimmicks: thin lines, calm colors, instant readability

ALWAYS ON. ALWAYS COMPLETE.

In always-on mode you keep the FULL watch face. Every dial stays visible, dimmed to half brightness, with built-in burn-in protection for your AMOLED screen.
