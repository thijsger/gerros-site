Earned

Burned calories, shown as food you can picture

Get it on Connect IQ ↗$1.99 / €2.49one-time purchase

What it does

Turn the calories you burn into something you can actually picture: a coffee, a slice of pizza, a beer.

Pick a food or drink and the dial shows how many of them today's burn has paid for. Swipe up to see what is still missing — in calories, and in minutes of running or walking.

  • 265 foods and drinks across 14 categories, each a realistic serving
  • Every list shows what an item costs and how many you have earned
  • Counts active calories only, so sitting still does not buy you cake (switchable to total burn)
  • Recent picks stay one tap away
  • Glance view shows your count without opening the app

No account, no internet connection, no subscription.
