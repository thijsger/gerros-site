Spent

Daily budget: what you may still spend today.

★ 5.0 · 1 review · version 1.0.4

Open in the Connect IQ Store

Spent puts your daily budget on your wrist. Log an expense in three taps, right at the checkout, and see at a glance what you can still spend today. No phone, no bank login, nothing leaves your watch.

Set a monthly or weekly budget and Spent divides what is left over the days that remain, every single day. Spend less today and tomorrow gets a bit more; overshoot and the number turns red. A thin ring shows how much of today you have used.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Cash-register keypad: type 350 and get 3.50, no decimal key needed
  • 12 categories, sorted by how often you use them, plus your own categories
  • Repeat a recent expense with one tap
  • Monthly budget with your own start day (for example payday) or a weekly budget
  • Safe-to-spend today, recalculated daily from what is left in the period
  • Last 14 days as a bar chart with your daily average line
  • Categories page with the biggest spenders this period
  • Period page: spent, budget, forecast for the end of the period, days under budget
  • Edit or delete any expense, history of closed periods
  • 21 currencies, correct decimals, glance with today's number
  • English, Dutch, German, Spanish and French

WHAT IT NEEDS

A touchscreen watch. Everything is stored on the watch itself.
