Listo

Grocery list in colour, sorted by aisle

Get it on Connect IQ ↗$1.99 / €2.49one-time purchase

What it does

Listo puts your grocery list on your wrist, in colour. Every aisle has its own colour: the ring on the home screen is your route through the store, the ticks in the list tell you where you are, and one tap marks a product as bought, hands on the cart and phone in your pocket.

Listo also thinks ahead. It learns how often you buy each product and tells you what is running low before you notice. It learns prices and estimates what the list will cost. And it times every shopping trip.

What It Does

  • Catalogue of 300+ everyday products in 10 aisles, no typing needed
  • Running low: predicted from your own buying rhythm, add everything in one tap
  • Sets: pasta night, breakfast, tacos, BBQ, cleaning day and more, or save your own list as a set
  • Tap to add, tap again for one more; any quantity and unit (pieces, kg, g, L, pack)
  • Budget: learned prices per product, estimated total, a limit per list, your currency
  • Shopping trips: how long each trip took, how many items, what it cost, and averages
  • Store route: put the aisles in the order of your own supermarket
  • Several lists, each with its own name and budget
  • Bring back last trip's items with one tap, or sort by aisle, by order added or A to Z
  • Type anything else on the watch; typed items stay one tap away under My own items
  • Add items from your phone in the Garmin Connect app settings ("2 kg apples, milk, 6 eggs")
  • Glance with items left and a colour bar per aisle
  • English, Dutch, German, Spanish and French, including the whole catalogue

What It Needs

Nothing but your watch. No account, no phone in the store.
