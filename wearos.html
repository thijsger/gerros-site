Wear OS

The same apps, native on Wear OS.

Every GerrOS app started on Garmin. The Wear OS versions are not ports of a port: each one is rebuilt in Kotlin with Compose for Wear OS, with tiles and complications where they make sense, and the same maths underneath. They will arrive in the Play Store under the GerrOS name.

Caffi

Caffeine curve with a sleep cut-off, a tile for the watch face and a complication.

Tile done · complication in progress

Sobr

Blood-alcohol estimate that keeps counting in the background, with alarms instead of polling.

App, tile and icon done · testing

BreathGym

CO₂ and O₂ tables, max-hold tests and a tile that shows today's training.

App and tile done · testing

Convertr

Units and live currency rates, the whole table ported one to one.

App done · live rates working
